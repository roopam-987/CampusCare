ABOUT CAMPUSCARE

Your Campus. Your Voice. Our Responsibility.

CampusCare is a campus issue reporting and maintenance management platform designed for MIT Academy of Engineering. It brings students, administrators and maintenance workers together through one organized digital system.

01

Easy Issue Reporting

Students can report cleanliness, infrastructure, electrical and other campus problems with descriptions and photos.

02

Location Assistance

Add the issue location manually or use device location support to help the maintenance team find the problem.

03

Transparent Tracking

Follow the status of submitted reports and receive updates as the maintenance team works on them.

HOW IT WORKS

Three Steps. One Better Campus.

From reporting a problem to getting it fixed, CampusCare makes the process simple and organized.

01
📝

Report

Students submit a report with its category, description, photo and location.

02
🛠️

Assign

Administrators review the issue, set its priority and assign it to the appropriate worker.

03
✅

Resolve

Workers complete the assigned task. Administrators verify the work and close the report.

WHAT CAN YOU REPORT?

Every Campus Issue Matters.

Report different types of maintenance and infrastructure problems from one place.

🧹

Cleanliness

Garbage, dirty spaces and sanitation.

💡

Electrical

Broken lights, wiring and power problems.

🏢

Infrastructure

Damaged buildings, walls and facilities.

🚰

Water & Plumbing

Water leakage and plumbing problems.

🪑

Classroom Issues

Furniture, boards and classroom facilities.

⚠️

Safety Concerns

Hazards and potentially dangerous situations.
